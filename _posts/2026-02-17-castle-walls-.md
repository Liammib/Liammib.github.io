> **Game Engine/Language**: Lönn

> **Role**: Solo Dev

> **Duration of Work**: 4 Weeks

> **End Of Project**: 17th February 2026

This is a map that I made in Celeste for my Experimental Level Design module. We had many games to pick from to make a level in but I chose Celeste because it is one of my favourite games.

I focused on mechanic led design, keeping the WaveDashing mechanic at the forefront of all of my level design choices. 

To make this, I needed to learn Lönn which was a challenge but became second nature towards the end of the project.

I also made sure the level looked aesthetically nice when making it and tied in features of the map editor such as stylegrounds and tile palettes.
