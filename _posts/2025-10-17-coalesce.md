> **Game Engine/Language**: Unity 6000.1.11f1 / C#

> **Role**: Solo Dev

> **Duration of Work**: 8 Weeks
 
> **End Of Project**: 17th October 2025

Coalesce is an arcade game made in my Arcade Game Production module at the University of Staffordshire. 

One player pilots the neon blue square character to platform through the levels of the game while the other player uses the SINDEN Light Gun to disable walls and traps to work together to complete the level.

I made the mechanics, designed and built the levels, created the visual effects and created all the pixel art for the game.

There are three unique levels which ramp slowly in difficulty and contrain unique mechanics for the gun and square to interact with.
